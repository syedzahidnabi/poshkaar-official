# Poshkaar Kashmir Organic Visibility Playbook

This is the zero-ad-spend plan for making `poshkaarkashmir` show up consistently across search, social, maps, AI answers and customer sharing.

## Brand Rule

Use the same name everywhere:

- Display name: Poshkaar Kashmir
- Search handle: poshkaarkashmir
- Website: https://poshkaarkashmir.com
- Instagram: @posh__kaar
- Email: poshkaarkashmirofficial@gmail.com

## Free Channels To Claim

- Google Search Console: add `https://poshkaarkashmir.com`, submit `/sitemap.xml`, request indexing for the homepage, shop, collections and top products.
- Google Business Profile: create or update the Srinagar business profile with website, phone, WhatsApp and product photos.
- Bing Webmaster Tools: import from Google Search Console and submit the same sitemap.
- Pinterest business profile: use `poshkaarkashmir` in display name and bio, then pin every product image to boards for Pashmina, Tilla, Aari, Dabka, Kashmir gifts and bridal couture.
- Instagram bio: include `poshkaarkashmir.com` in bio and use consistent captions with `Poshkaar Kashmir` and `poshkaarkashmir`.
- YouTube Shorts: post 15-30 second craft/process clips with titles starting `Poshkaar Kashmir`.
- LinkedIn company page: create a brand page for trust signals and backlinks.
- Reddit/Quora answers: answer genuinely helpful questions about Kashmiri embroidery, pashmina care, gifting and wedding wear. Link only when useful.

## Weekly Content Rhythm

- Monday: one product post with title, price, material, size and `poshkaarkashmir.com`.
- Wednesday: one craft education post, such as Tilla vs Aari, Sozni care, Dabka occasionwear.
- Friday: one short video showing handwork, packaging, fabric detail or customer styling.
- Sunday: one story poll or WhatsApp prompt: custom size, colour request, wedding gifting, winter wear.

## Caption Formula

Use this structure:

`Poshkaar Kashmir | [product/craft name]`

`A [material/category] piece for [occasion/use]. Available through poshkaarkashmir.com.`

`DM or WhatsApp for custom sizing.`

Hashtags:

`#poshkaarkashmir #PoshkaarKashmir #KashmirCraft #KashmiriEmbroidery #Pashmina #TillaWork #AariWork #Dabka #KashmiriFashion #IndianLuxury`

## Backlink Targets

- Kashmir craft blogs
- Wedding directories
- Indian handmade product lists
- Local Srinagar/Kashmir directories
- Fashion student blogs and craft documentation pages
- Instagram creator bio links after collaborations or product seeding

## Product Page Habit

Every product should include:

- Product name with craft term
- Material
- Origin
- Care
- Occasion
- At least one real image
- WhatsApp/order path

This gives search engines a reason to rank individual product pages, not only the homepage.
